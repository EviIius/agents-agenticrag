Which of my recipes has the least cleanup, and why?

One-pot chicken Alfredo is the lower-cleanup choice. It cooks and serves in a single wide skillet or Dutch oven1, and rotisserie chicken avoids a separate raw-meat prep board and pan1.

Sheet-pan nachos come close. Lining the pan with parchment makes cleanup easier2, though the wet toppings go on only after the pan leaves the oven2.
