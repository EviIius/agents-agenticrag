My library/Least-cleanup recipe
Which of my recipes has the least cleanup, and why?
Agentic·Gemma 4 12B·11.6 s Review passed · no unsupported claims

One-pot chicken Alfredo is the lower-cleanup choice. It cooks and serves in a single wide skillet or Dutch oven, and rotisserie chicken avoids a separate raw-meat prep board and pan. The note also suggests washing the small prep bowls while the pasta simmers.

Sheet-pan nachos come close. Lining the pan with parchment makes cleanup easier, though the wet toppings go on only after the pan leaves the oven.
