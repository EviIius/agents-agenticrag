### Why the candidate still stops

The remainder completes three grounded streams: two basketball turns and the
first capital-city turn. The subsequent “thanks” turn uses the existing ordinary
skipped-search path. The next grounded request, `tailscale-serve`, selects six
legal text bundles. Five belong to source 1; their standalone prose costs are
13, 12, 14, 18 and 17 words. A sixth costs 16 words at source 2. Shared context
must be deduplicated, so these displayed costs are not a general sum formula;
the unchanged cumulative validator nevertheless rejects the actual combination.

The native references/bundle names are owned and individually admitted. This is
not an ignored grammar or invented-value finding. It demonstrates that individual
admission does not solve global budget planning. The host rejects with `Public
source quotation/data budget exceeded` before yielding any factual text from
that response. The child exits 1, the driver retains the failure index and blocks
Library. No selected subset was used to repair or regrade the failed answer.

Five grounded selector streams ran in total, including the original smoke and
the rejected remainder stream. There is one complete one-case report, and no
full Web report or full-suite score. The other accepted streams still contain
unneeded coach/game details and literal source formatting; structural provenance
does not establish useful brevity or relevance. Retained logs show 9.84, 16.52
and 16.99-second TTFTs for those three answers; incomplete-run timings do not
establish E13's full-run median or successful latency qualification.

## Decision and next steps

**Stop this selector design here.** Individual bundle admission fixes the diagnosed
smoke mismatch, but does not make a reliable generally useful answer system.
Another schema/repair iteration is outside this bounded checkpoint.

For reliability, review using the existing source cards/“What the model saw”
presentation to expose useful retrieved evidence when support is uncertain,
with explicit missing-evidence status. Preserve original context and avoid a
“verified” badge based on syntax or model agreement. This is a proposed simpler
product behavior, not an implemented fallback or proof of factual correctness.
Existing factual workflow proposals remain reviewable drafts until qualified.

Next independent roadmap checkpoint is the named local image trial in
`IMAGE-TRIAL-PLAN.md`: proposed MFLUX/klein 4B worker, frozen synthetic prompts,
resource measurements, actual progress/cancellation and quality review. The
dependency/worker choice is concrete for review; no image installation occurred.
