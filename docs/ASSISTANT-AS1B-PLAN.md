## Qualification and stopping

Body reads first check AX character counts (at most 4,000 per area / 16,000 total)
and require bounded `AXStringForRange` support; no fallback to unbounded AXValue
copying. The UTF-8 limits above are acceptance limits. Attribute APIs for titles,
identifiers and descriptions return complete attributes before size validation;
oversized results are discarded privately. Do not claim the caps prevent every
transient oversized attribute copy. Settable flags describe the AX API, not the
semantic role of a control. No candidate is automatically called a message.

Apple documents [attribute reads and unsupported-attribute errors](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/applicationservices/1462085-axuielementcopyattributevalue)
and [the value-modifiability check](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/applicationservices/1459972-axuielementisattributesettable).
Those contracts do not prove this app exposes message/recipient identity.

The receipt is explicitly **unqualified candidate evidence**, not a
`DesktopObservation.conversation`. Do not infer sender/direction from text
content, invent an address from a display name, or invent persistent IDs from
paths/structure hashes. No parser is connected to the app or model. If the
attributes do not establish direct recipient, thread, message order and latest
incoming identity, stop with that gap and propose a specific bridge remedy.
The selected self-thread may have no incoming message in the bounded slice;
report that limited state rather than asserting no incoming history exists.

AS1-B1 is a feasibility read, not permission for a completed native driver,
model trial or send. A changed build's permission identity, actual cancellation,
changed-thread negative control and a complete qualified adapter still need
their reviewed evidence before AS2. Preserve all outcomes and the original
AS1-A modal refusal. Do not reuse its remaining slot or AS1-A2's exhausted ledger.
