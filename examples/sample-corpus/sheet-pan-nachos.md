# Sheet-pan nachos for a quick meal

This original sample note gives the workbench a second cooking source for retrieval tests.

## Ingredients

- A large bag of sturdy tortilla chips
- 2 cups shredded cheese that melts well
- 1 can black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 cup cooked chicken or another already-cooked topping, optional
- Diced tomato, sliced jalapeño, cilantro, sour cream, and lime for serving

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 400°F (205°C). Line a sheet pan with parchment for easier cleanup.
2. Spread half the chips in one layer. Add half the cheese, beans, and any cooked chicken.
3. Add the remaining chips, cheese, and beans. Keep wet toppings off until after baking so the chips stay crisp.
4. Bake for about 8 to 12 minutes, until the cheese melts and the edges of the chips are lightly toasted.
5. Add tomato, jalapeño, cilantro, sour cream, and lime after the pan leaves the oven.
