# One-pot chicken Alfredo with little cleanup

This is a sample cooking note for testing local document search. It is an original example, not a professional culinary standard.

## Ingredients for four servings

- 12 ounces dry fettuccine or another pasta
- 2 cups cooked rotisserie chicken, shredded
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 cups low-sodium chicken broth
- 1 cup water, with more available if the pan dries out
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 1 cup finely grated Parmesan
- Salt and black pepper to taste
- Optional: chopped parsley

## Minimal-mess method

1. In a wide deep skillet or Dutch oven, melt the butter and warm the garlic for about 30 seconds.
2. Add broth, water, and dry pasta. Simmer, stirring regularly, until the pasta is nearly tender. Add a little water if needed so the pasta remains mostly submerged.
3. Reduce the heat. Stir in the cream and cooked chicken until warmed through.
4. Turn off the heat and add Parmesan a handful at a time. Stir until the sauce coats the pasta. Season to taste.

Use the same pan for cooking and serving. A rotisserie chicken avoids a separate raw-meat prep board and pan. Put garlic, cheese, and other ingredients in small bowls before starting, then wash those bowls while the pasta simmers.
